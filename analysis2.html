Analysis of Average Salary

This map of the United States provides us the cities that the dataset contains job listings for. We see that major cities like San Francisco, Chicago, Pittsburgh, and New York are among the highest in job listings.

This graph displays the average salary across the different US cities. We see that among the top cities, most of them are located in California. The highest average salary is listed in the city of Lake Forest, IL.

This pie chart shows the division of job listings and the average salary for the different types of ownership. We see that Public Companies account for 32% of the listing population, followed by Private Companies at 28%, followed by Non-profit Organizations at 22%, and Subsidiary or Business Segments at 17%.

Here we see a bar graph of average salaries across the industries. The top industries are Biotech & Pharmaceuticals, Insurance Carriers, and Computer Hardware and Software.

We see from this pie chart the average salary across Seniority levels. Senior positions have the top average salary at 46% of the job listing population, followed by positions that don't list seniority level at 33%, and junior positions at 21%.

Finally, this word cloud provides us the position titles that occur frequently within the job listing population.
